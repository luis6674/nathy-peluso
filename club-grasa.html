CLUB GRASA is a party. I've always believed that dancing is a great remedy for the soul.

Through these parties I want to amplify my desire to connect through music in all its forms, to make room for people I admire, and to amplify different cultures and rhythms. At Club Grasa, DJs and producers from every corner serve up bangers like offerings. Every date is an unrepeatable experience: a traveling party where irreverent, bold, free, and authentic people dance on stage without rules.

CLUB GRASA is endless celebration. We have to dance to heal our hearts.
