Nathy Peluso — Bio

Since the release of her debut album Calambre in 2020, Argentine singer, rapper, and songwriter Nathy Peluso, now based in Spain, has become one of the most distinctive forces in Latin music, captivating audiences with her genre-defying artistry and commanding stage presence. At just 30 years old, she has won five Latin GRAMMY Awards, tying Mercedes Sosa as the most awarded Argentine female artist in the history of the Latin GRAMMYs. She also made history as the first woman ever to win the award for Best Rap/Hip-Hop Song. To date, she has received a total of 16 Latin GRAMMY nominations.

Renowned for her electrifying live performances and fearless artistic vision, Nathy has performed at some of the world's most iconic venues and festivals, including Coachella, the Hollywood Bowl, Movistar Arena, Sónar, and Mad Cool. On July 15, 2026, she headlined a landmark performance alongside the legendary Colombian orchestra Grupo Niche at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles, one of the world's most prestigious venues. The concert reaffirmed her deep connection to salsa and further cemented her place on the international stage. Her collaboration with Bizarrap (BZRP Music Sessions #36) became a global phenomenon, amassing hundreds of millions of streams and significantly expanding her international reach.

In 2024, she released her second studio album, GRASA, an ambitious and deeply personal body of work that blends rap, salsa, ballads, and tropical influences, inspired by classic mafia films and the New York salsa movement of the 1970s. The album features collaborations with artists including Blood Orange, Duki, C. Tangana, Ca7riel, and Paco Amoroso, marking a defining moment in her artistic evolution. GRASA earned three Latin GRAMMY Awards and received a nomination for Best Latin Rock or Alternative Album at the 2025 GRAMMY Awards. That same year, she introduced CLUB GRASA, an EP of electronic reinterpretations of her musical universe, and made her debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon with a critically acclaimed performance alongside Blood Orange.

Between 2024 and 2025, Nathy embarked on her first world tour, the GRASA Tour, selling out shows in cities including New York, Los Angeles, London, and Paris, further establishing herself as one of the leading voices in contemporary music.

In February 2025, she released EROTIKA, a tribute to the explosive New York salsa erótica scene of the 1990s. The project marked the beginning of a new creative chapter that continued with MALPORTADA, her collaboration with Rawayana, and later with the MALPORTADA EP, a six-track project written in Puerto Rico that reaffirmed her profound connection to salsa. With raw energy and an uncompromising artistic voice, Nathy reimagines the genre through her own perspective, combining bold lyricism with exceptional musicality.

In February 2026, Nathy Peluso reached another milestone with the release of "Como en el Idilio," a classic salsa collaboration with Marc Anthony. This historic partnership not only confirmed her place within the lineage of salsa music but also highlighted her ability to stand alongside one of the genre's greatest icons, building a bridge between tradition and innovation.

Today, Nathy continues to expand her creative universe through CLUB GRASA, a project that began as a remix EP and has evolved into a traveling club experience curated and hosted by the artist herself. More than a concert, CLUB GRASA is a celebration of global club culture, bringing together DJs, producers, and selectors from around the world to reinterpret her music, while Nathy takes the stage as host, MC, and performer, sharing the spotlight with an ever-evolving community of guest artists and dancers.
